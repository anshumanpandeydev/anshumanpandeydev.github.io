I'm a full-stack developer who ships complete products.

Frontend, backend, database, deployment — and increasingly, the AI layer that makes them smart. Over the last two years I've built and delivered agri-tech, e-commerce, SaaS, and marketing platforms, working across React, Node.js, Python, and four different LLM providers.

My flagship work is AskDocs, a self-hosted document-intelligence platform where I've written 130 of its 158 commits — hybrid retrieval, OCR ingestion, an MCP server, A2A and a local agent service. Before that I led BhuMitra, an agri-tech platform (99 of 122 commits) with a 16-tool LLM agent and a dual ML+LLM prediction engine. On the BazaarBandhu wholesale platform, a production NestJS microservices system, I designed and built the notification system end to end, and I own the analytics and wallet modules.

I use AI pair-programming tools heavily and deliberately — they multiply my output while I own every architecture and integration decision. What I care about: systems that degrade gracefully, validate at boundaries, and actually ship.
